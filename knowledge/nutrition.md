# Nutrition: calories

## Burgers

Classic burger 520 calories; with cheese add 70. Cheeseburger 590. Double
cheeseburger 830. Crispy chicken burger 610. Veggie burger 480.

## Sides

Fries: small 230, medium 340, large 480 calories. Onion rings: regular 360,
large 540. Chicken nuggets: 6 pieces 280, 9 pieces 420, 12 pieces 560, not
counting the dip. Side salad 70 calories before dressing; ranch adds 140 and
vinaigrette 90.

## Drinks

Cola: small 150, medium 210, large 290 calories. Lemon-lime soda about the same.
Sweetened iced tea: small 90, medium 130, large 180; unsweetened has none.
Orange juice: small 150, large 250. Black coffee has under 5 calories.

## Desserts

Soft serve cone 200 calories. Sundae 330 to 380 depending on the topping.
Milkshake: small 520, large 780. Apple pie 240. Chocolate chip cookie 170.
