# The veggie burger

## What the patty is made of

The veggie patty is made from soy protein, wheat gluten, black beans,
vegetables, oats and spices. It contains no meat, fish or egg.

## Is the veggie burger vegetarian

Yes. The veggie burger has no meat or fish in it. It is cooked on its own side
of the grill with a separate spatula, but the grill itself is shared with the
beef patties.

## Is the veggie burger vegan

Not as it is served. The house sauce is made with egg, and the cheese option
contains milk. Ordered without house sauce and without cheese, it has no animal
ingredients, but it is cooked on a grill shared with beef and cheese, so we do
not call it vegan.

## Nuts and the veggie patty

The patty is made by a supplier whose kitchen also handles peanuts and tree
nuts, so it may contain traces of them.
