# Desserts and milkshakes

## Soft serve

Our soft serve, used in cones, sundaes and milkshakes, is made with milk,
cream, sugar and vanilla. It contains no egg. There is no dairy-free soft serve.

## Milkshake flavours

Milkshakes are soft serve blended with a syrup. The chocolate syrup contains
soy lecithin; the vanilla and strawberry syrups contain no priority allergens.

## Sundae toppings

Sundaes come with chocolate, caramel or strawberry topping. The topping station
also handles a seasonal nut topping, so sundaes may contain peanuts and tree
nuts.

## Apple pie

The apple pie has a baked pastry crust made with wheat flour, vegetable
shortening and soy. The filling is apples, sugar and cinnamon. It is baked in an
oven also used for pastries made with milk and egg.

## Chocolate chip cookie

The cookie is made with wheat flour, butter, eggs, sugar and chocolate chips
that contain soy. Cookies are baked by a bakery that also handles peanuts and
tree nuts.
