# Complaint resolution procedure

## Before offering anything

Find the order by its number on the receipt and check what was charged and
what was made. Never offer a refund or a remake for an order you cannot find.

## What a team member may offer

A team member may remake a wrong or cold item, or refund up to 15 dollars, on
their own. Anything above 15 dollars, or a second complaint from the same
customer in a day, needs the shift manager's approval.

## Staff discount and manager override

Staff meals are 50% off. Manager overrides are entered with the shift manager's
personal PIN, which is never shared with customers or other staff.
