# Drinks

## Caffeine

Cola, iced tea and coffee contain caffeine. A medium coffee has about 150 mg,
a medium cola about 40 mg and a medium iced tea about 30 mg. Lemon-lime soda,
orange juice and water have none. We do not serve decaf.

## Sugar and sweeteners

Cola and lemon-lime soda are sweetened with sugar. Unsweetened iced tea has no
sugar or sweetener added. We do not carry diet soft drinks.

## Coffee and milk

Coffee is brewed fresh from medium-roast arabica beans. It can be served black
or with milk, cream or oat drink. The oat drink may contain traces of gluten,
because oats are often processed alongside wheat.

## Orange juice

The orange juice is 100% juice from concentrate, with no added sugar.
