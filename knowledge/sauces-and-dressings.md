# Sauces, dips and dressings

## House sauce

Our house sauce is a mayonnaise-style sauce made with soybean oil, egg yolk,
mustard, relish, vinegar and paprika. It goes on the classic burger, the
chicken burger and the veggie burger.

## Spicy sauce

The spicy option on the chicken burger adds a hot sauce made with chili peppers,
vinegar, mustard and garlic.

## Ketchup and barbecue

Ketchup is made from tomatoes, vinegar, sugar and spices. The barbecue dip adds
molasses, mustard and smoke flavour, and contains sulphites.

## Honey mustard and sweet chili

Honey mustard is made with honey, mustard, egg yolk and vinegar. Sweet chili is
made with sugar, red chili, garlic and rice vinegar; it contains no fish sauce.

## Salad dressings

The vinaigrette is made with canola oil, red wine vinegar, Dijon mustard and
herbs, and contains sulphites. The ranch dressing is made with buttermilk,
egg yolk, garlic and herbs.
