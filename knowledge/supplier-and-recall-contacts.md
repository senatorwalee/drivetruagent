# Supplier and recall contacts

## Who to call about a recall

Call the district food safety lead first, then the supplier. Remove the
recalled product from the line and label it "do not use" until it is collected.

## Suppliers

Beef: Northfield Meats, account 4471. Buns: Lakeshore Bakery, account 2290.
Veggie patties: GreenPlate Foods, account 8812.
