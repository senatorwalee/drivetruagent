# Our kitchen, allergens and diets

## Can you guarantee an item is allergen-free

No. Our kitchen handles all the priority allergens, and grills, fryers and
counters are shared. We list what each item contains and what it may contain,
but we cannot guarantee that any item is free of an allergen. Customers with a
severe allergy are welcome to speak to a team member.

## Is the meat halal or kosher

Our beef and chicken are not certified halal or kosher.

## Is the beef real beef

Our burger patties are 100% Canadian beef, seasoned with salt and pepper on the
grill. They contain no fillers.

## Gluten

We have no gluten-free buns. Items without a bun still share a kitchen with
wheat, so we do not describe anything as gluten-free.
